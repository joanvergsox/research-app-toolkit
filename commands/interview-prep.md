Use the `interview-prep` skill to prepare for your research program interview with mock questions and feedback.

Arguments (optional):
- `professor-name` — name of the interviewing professor
- `university` — university name

Example: `/ra:interview-prep John Smith MIT`

If no arguments are provided, you will be asked for the professor name and university.
